Kurenai and Maccy

Maccy is a great clipboard manager and it's free too. If it does everything you need, keep it. Kurenai exists because I wanted a few things on top: pasting a batch of items in order, keeping lists of phrases I paste all the time, and a history file that isn't plain text.

Where Kurenai is different

  • Paste queue. Press ⌘', copy several things, then each ⌘V pastes the next one. ⇧⌘C turns a selected column into separate items, handy for spreadsheets and web forms.
  • Saved lists (⌘I) for replies, addresses, templates. A digit pastes a line.
  • History and images are encrypted on disk.
  • Its windows are marked private, so they stay out of screenshots and Telegram screen sharing.

Where Maccy is ahead

  • Pinned items. Kurenai doesn't have them yet, they're planned for the next version.
  • Maccy has been around for years and is on the Mac App Store. Kurenai is new and isn't notarized yet, so the first launch needs a click in Privacy & Security.

Both are open source, so trying Kurenai costs nothing. Your Maccy history stays where it is.
